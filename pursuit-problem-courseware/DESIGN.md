## Style Prompt

Bright classroom explainer video for a middle-school math concept. Use a clean worksheet-like canvas, strong motion diagrams, and concise formula callouts. The tone should feel clear, friendly, and practical rather than playful or decorative.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#f7faf7`
- Primary text: `#18324a`
- Muted text: `#546376`
- Teaching teal: `#0f766e`
- Speed orange: `#f97316`
- Accessible orange text: `#c2410c`
- Formula blue: `#2563eb`
- Soft panel: `#ffffff`

## Typography

- Use the renderer's generic `sans-serif` fallback for stable capture.
- Large headings are bold and compact; captions are medium weight with generous line height.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use dark cinematic backgrounds.
- Do not crowd the frame with long paragraphs.
- Do not use a one-color palette.
- Do not hide the formula inside small text.
- Do not make decorative elements compete with the motion diagram.
