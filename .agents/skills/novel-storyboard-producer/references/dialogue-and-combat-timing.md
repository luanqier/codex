# Dialogue and Combat Timing

## Source-driven chapter duration

Do not impose a default total duration or fixed segment count on a chapter. Derive the runtime after building the source event ledger.

Use 15 seconds as the default duration of every generation segment. Accept a user-specified segment duration as the override. Keep the number of segments dynamic so the complete chapter runtime can expand with the source.

Calculate the working duration from:

1. Dialogue at a natural speaking pace.
2. Emotional pauses, reactions, and information-processing beats.
3. Required visible actions and cause-and-effect transitions.
4. Establishing time needed to make a new location or confrontation geography readable.
5. A 30–45 second allowance for each story-significant large-scale confrontation.

## Dynamic shot count and non-uniform shot timing

Determine shot count only after identifying the Segment's actual visual beats. Start a new shot when the story needs a materially new composition, speaker emphasis, reaction, action phase, reveal, spatial orientation, scale change, or transition. Keep a beat in the same shot when camera movement and blocking can express it clearly without sacrificing readability. Do not target a preset shot-count range or design the script around a preferred storyboard grid.

Calculate every shot duration independently:

1. Measure dialogue at the character's natural speaking pace, including meaningful pauses and interruptions.
2. Add the time required for the visible action to begin, develop, and reach a readable endpoint.
3. Add only the motivated reaction, gaze, breath, posture, or object interaction needed for performance.
4. Allow enough time for camera travel, focus change, reveal comprehension, environmental response, and sound decay.
5. Keep inserts, cutaways, and simple reactions shorter than dialogue-heavy, multi-phase action, reveal, or long-travel shots when the content supports that difference.

Do not divide the Segment duration by the number of shots. Do not assign every shot the same duration or repeat a mechanical pattern across Segments. Adjust the independently estimated values until their sum matches the Segment duration while preserving their relative timing needs. If two or more shots genuinely require the same duration, vary the surrounding shots and record a rationale when the entire Segment would otherwise become uniform.

Respect an explicit user or project duration as an override. If that cap cannot contain the required events and dialogue naturally, report the conflict and propose the smallest viable runtime instead of silently deleting content.

## Dialogue retention

- Preserve safe source dialogue as fully as practical, especially promises, revelations, relationship changes, tactical information, recurring lines, and chapter hooks.
- Budget runtime around meaningful dialogue rather than compressing the dialogue to fit an arbitrary runtime.
- Allow natural pauses for emotion, reaction, interruption, and speaker changes.
- Compress only repeated wording, redundant exposition, or content that can be shown visually without losing intent or information.
- Automatically rewrite prohibited or insulting wording into safe, dignified language without repeating the original wording. Never invent replacement dialogue that changes motivation, knowledge, relationship, or outcome.

## Safe large-scale confrontation contract

When the source contains a story-significant fight, safely adapt it into a coherent 30–45 second large-scale xuanhuan/xianxia cultivation-level high-dynamic but non-graphic confrontation. At the default segment duration, divide it into two or three consecutive 15-second segments. A deliberately instant resolution or trivial encounter may remain shorter only when brevity is itself a required source event.

Treat 30–45 seconds as the full confrontation sequence, not one generation segment. If the user specifies another segment duration, repartition it while preserving the full sequence and continuous action state.

Build the sequence from story-supported beats:

1. Establish participants, objective, stakes, and readable spatial geography.
2. Open with a distinctive technique display, evasive move, barrier response, pursuit, or positional advantage.
3. Escalate through varied movement, authorized powers, artifacts, formations, or safe environmental interaction.
4. Introduce a reversal, adaptation, reveal, reduced energy state, or tactical change.
5. Deliver the decisive non-contact energy resolution and its safe visible consequence.
6. End on a safely adapted version of the source outcome, reaction, or hook.

Use cultivation-scale dynamics through acceleration and deceleration, aerial-to-ground movement when source-supported, directional camera movement, extreme scale changes, technique or energy-field interaction, pressure waves, safe terrain transformation, light refraction, protective barriers, and clear character-driven movement. Make the space feel vast and the power level visually consequential while keeping actions readable. Keep faces, costumes, artifacts, techniques, power stages, positions, and ownership continuous.

Magnify only what the source or project bible authorizes. Do not invent a cultivation stage, supernatural ability, technique name, magical artifact, summon, formation, transformation, harmful outcome, winner, or consequence merely to reach xianxia spectacle. If the source uses another power system, translate the scale and dynamics into that system without importing incompatible cultivation lore.

Do not pad the sequence with repetitive exchanges, interchangeable bursts, random abilities, excessive motion blur, bodily impact, suffering, or effects that hide the action. Do not add a winner, harmful outcome, power, transformation, or plot result absent from the safely adapted source event.

## Timing QA

Before approving the script, verify:

- The derived chapter runtime equals the sum of all segment durations.
- Every segment is exactly 15 seconds by default or exactly matches the user's stated override.
- Dialogue has enough speaking and reaction time.
- Shot count is justified by actual visual beats rather than a preset range or preferred grid.
- Shot durations were calculated per shot and are not mechanically equal or template-repeated.
- No meaningful dialogue was removed solely to reach a preset length.
- Every story-significant confrontation receives 30–45 seconds of large-scale cultivation-level but non-graphic spectacle unless the source explicitly requires an instant outcome.
- At the default setting, the fight is partitioned into two or three linked 15-second segments.
- Linked confrontation segments preserve geography, action state, props, powers, energy state, and safely adapted outcome.
- Every confrontation beat advances tactics, stakes, character, or outcome rather than merely filling time.
